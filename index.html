About

Empowering People Through Practical Technology Education

Freedom Skill Hub was founded with a vision to make modern technology and artificial intelligence accessible to students, graduates, working professionals and entrepreneurs. Technology is evolving rapidly, and we believe learning should evolve with it. Our focus is to simplify complex technologies and teach them through practical, hands-on learning.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional education and the practical technology skills required in the modern workplace. Our vision is to build a learning community where people confidently learn, experiment, innovate and grow with technology.

Meet the Founder

I am Sushma N H, an Electrical Engineering graduate with a passion for technology, education and innovation. My journey began as an Electrical Engineer and grew through teaching and lecturing, which taught me to simplify complex concepts.
